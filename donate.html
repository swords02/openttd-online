Transparency notes

OpenTTD Online is a community-friendly project built on top of the open-source OpenTTD ecosystem. Donations support the website, the browser port, and iOS development work around the project.

  • No paywalls. The game stays free to play.
  • Donations are used for operational costs and project growth.
  • OpenTTD itself is open source and credited appropriately.
